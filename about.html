Who We Are

Your situation deserves to be understood before anyone offers an answer. You can expect us to listen carefully and be candid about what we see. We stay open to what we may be missing, too.

Steve Gallagher

Founder

Steve believes people often know more about their next step than they realize. His work is to listen closely, ask questions that open up possibilities, and help them decide how to move forward.

Across 25 years in higher education, he has worked alongside students, families, and colleagues through decisions both personal and institutional. His roles have included Dean of Students and Interim Vice President and Chief Student Affairs Officer. At ideate, he brings that experience to a simple purpose: helping people find a way forward they can make their own.

Matt Huett

Principal Coach & Consultant

Matt helps leaders see connections that are easy to miss. He makes sense of complex information and relates it to what’s happening in society, helping institutional leaders anticipate cultural shifts and plan their response. Leaders consistently seek him out for that perspective.

His experience spans spiritual formation, creative consulting for test-pilot productions at Food Network, and pastoral leadership in New York City, Florida, and North Carolina.

Every point of view leaves something out. Our missing ray is a reminder to stay curious about what we’re not seeing yet.
