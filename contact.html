About working together

How long would we work together?

That depends on the work. We agree on the pace and review points with you, whether the need is a defined consulting assignment or ongoing individual support.

How do you choose assessments?

We determine which instruments will give us a useful understanding of where you are currently, guided by your circumstances and what you want to work on.

The EQ-i 2.0, for example, explores your own view of how you understand and express emotions, relate to others, make decisions, and respond to stress. It can help us examine questions such as whether you communicate your needs directly, how you handle pressure, or how emotions influence a difficult decision.

We discuss the findings with you in the context of your experience. That understanding helps us choose what to work on, put it into practice, and revisit what you’re learning.

What stays private?

Before work begins, we agree on confidentiality and any expectations for reporting to an employer or other sponsor.
